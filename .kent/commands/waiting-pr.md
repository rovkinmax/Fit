# Wait for Human PR Disposition

Use the configured `wait_pr` helper and native Waiting PR/Merge Watch routes.
Observe the exact repository, PR head and declared base `main`. Preserve
provider-read constraints and native evidence carriers.

Do not merge or enable auto-merge. A human merge requires fresh merged/base
identity evidence before Cleanup. Closed-unmerged, unavailable or conflicting
observations are not successful delivery; follow the declared blocked,
feedback or cancellation route with exact evidence.
